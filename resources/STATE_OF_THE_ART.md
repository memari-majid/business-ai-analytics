# State of the art in AI and business analytics

**Evidence reviewed November 18, 2026.** This is a snapshot of methods, business use, and evidence. For a specific problem, use the [topic index](../learning/TOPIC_INDEX.md). For sources to keep up with new work, see the [research feeds](ONLINE.md#follow-new-research) and [people directory](PEOPLE.md).

## What is established and what is advancing

| Area | Established practice | Research frontier | What to verify for a real decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Data and business intelligence** | Define grain, join keys, time windows, and metric denominators. Reconcile dashboard measures against source records. | Copilots combine SQL, calculations, and retrieval across tables and documents. The DataSpace benchmark scores whether data agents return a complete, verifiable tabular result from a heterogeneous workspace. | Test which metric, period, and entities an assistant selected. Verify intermediate counts and trace important figures to their source. [Star-schema guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/guidance/star-schema) · [DataSpace preprint](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.03451) |
| **Tabular prediction** | Use interpretable rules or logistic models as baselines, then compare tree ensembles or other learners on a realistic holdout. Evaluate calibration and the decision threshold as well as ranking. | Pretrained tabular models such as TabPFN can be competitive on benchmark tasks in small and moderate datasets. | Use a holdout reflecting the future population and the data available at decision time. Compare cost-weighted errors and how gains change across cohorts. [TabPFN study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08328-6) · [Decision economics references](BOOKS.md#business-decisions-and-graduate-analytics) |
| **Forecasting and optimization** | Compare seasonal baselines and tuned models on historical forecast origins. Use uncertainty and asymmetric costs to choose an order, allocation, or staffing level. | Multivariate time-series foundation models can use related series and external inputs. Contextual forecasting benchmarks examine whether models use forward-looking information such as planned events. | Use identical horizons and information for every model. Check calibration and evaluate the downstream policy on stockouts, costs, or service levels. [Chronos-2 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41956) · [GIFT-Eval-CTX preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17794) · [Inventory lab](../labs/02_inventory_decision.py) |
| **Experiments and causal ML** | Use randomized assignment where feasible. Define the treatment, comparator, estimand, guardrails, and economic threshold before looking at the result. For observational designs, justify identification. | Researchers combine historical causal evidence with embeddings to evaluate new actions described in text. A recent digital experimentation roundtable discusses using experimental, observational, and synthetic evidence for different questions. | Put effect heterogeneity and support on the same footing as model fit. Validate on completely held-out treatments and collect new evidence for actions far from the ones observed. [Ellickson et al.](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437261476639) · [November 2026 roundtable preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16335) · [Campaign lab](../labs/01_campaign_decision.ipynb) |
| **Text, customer research, and retrieval** | Build an auditable classification or extraction baseline. Validate labels against human examples. For question answering, retrieve policy or document evidence that applies to the user and time period. | Businesses combine multimodal features, retrieval, generation, and simulated respondents. A synthetic-respondent preprint found that aggregate agreement with human responses could mask weak respondent-level discrimination. | Match validation to the decision. Audit subgroup and item-level errors, source dates, claim support, and whether the system abstains when evidence is missing. [Wharton–Petco case](https://ai-analytics.wharton.upenn.edu/student-programs/analytics-accelerator/optimizing-petcos-email-marketing-strategy-using-generative-ai/) · [Netzer and Sambandam preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13995) · [Grounded Q&A lab](../labs/03_grounded_policy_qa.py) |
| **Agents and workflow evaluation** | Separate reading data, calculating, drafting, acting, and approving. Grade exact business checks in code where possible and review trade-offs separately. | Recent benchmarks evaluate end-to-end tasks, including strategic reasoning and actions scored against simulated business consequences. BusinessCaseBench applies instructor rubrics to model answers to business cases. | Report full-task pass rates, failure categories, and results across repeated runs. A correct answer can coexist with bad actions or unsupported evidence. [Argo-Bench preprint](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.02122) · [BusinessCaseBench preprint](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.16057) · [Agent evaluation guide](https://www.anthropic.com/research/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents) |
| **Business value and deployment** | Compare with the incumbent workflow. Account for integration and human review, operational errors, adoption, permissions, monitoring, and rollback. | Field experiments increasingly measure outcomes in operating businesses. Usage studies describe tasks and adoption. Governance research offers ways to design and document controls. | Measure outcomes on both sides of the decision, such as resolution and repeat contact. Track total costs, risk, who benefits, and whether results persist. [Research on organizations and work](RESEARCH.md#organizations-and-real-work) · [NIST Generative AI Profile](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1) |

## What recent studies actually found

- **Fang et al., retail.** Seven randomized workflows at one cross-border retailer produced sales effects ranging from no detectable change to 16.3%, mainly through conversion. The results are short-run and depend on the workflow and its comparator. A sales lift needs a separate margin and cost calculation. [*Management Science*, November 2026](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2026.00623)
- **Dell’Acqua et al., consulting.** In a preregistered experiment with 758 consultants, GPT-4 improved speed and quality on selected tasks. On one difficult exercise, correctness was approximately 19 percentage points lower when pooling the GPT-4 arms against control. Figure 5 shows why a system needs checks for particular tasks and failure modes. [*Organization Science*, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1287/orsc.2025.21838) · [Figure 5](https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/dell-acqua-et-al-2026-navigating-the-jagged-technological-frontier_5c589c8c-fbb5-458f-b285-c944746cd717.pdf#page=12)
- **Choi et al., accounting.** A survey, field records, and a framed experiment describe how generative AI affects transaction categorization and other accounting tasks. Use the experimental comparisons for claims about task accuracy and the field observations for how workplaces use assistance. [*Journal of Accounting Research*, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1111/1475-679X.70052)
- **Tomitsuka et al., data agents.** On Argo-Bench, the strongest tested configuration earned at least 95/100 on 34.8% of 210 tasks. The study measures performance in a simulated food-delivery operation. Its error analysis includes cases where the agent used the wrong quantity or objective. [October 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.02122#S3)
- **Patel et al., business cases.** On BusinessCaseBench, Claude Sonnet 4.6 earned 88.4% average rubric credit and met every criterion on 49.6% of questions. These are measures of model performance on supplied case questions. The scoring design is useful when deciding what a business recommendation must demonstrate. [BusinessCaseBench preprint, v5](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.16057v5#S3)

## Questions the evidence leaves open

- **Value outside the pilot.** Which gains survive broader adoption, a longer horizon, changing demand, and full implementation costs? Study the existing process and measure quality, work shifted to reviewers, and the cost of mistakes.
- **Transfer across populations.** Do a model's forecasts, recommendations, or estimated effects hold for new stores, customers, or policies? Choose holdouts that match the proposed change and monitor performance by cohort.
- **Delegation and controls.** Which steps should software execute and which should a person verify? Evaluate both the recommended decision and any actions the system can take.
- **Learning with AI.** When a student uses AI to write a case answer, can they defend its assumptions, recreate the key calculation, and revise their decision when new evidence arrives? Give those skills their own measures. See the [business-school teaching map](../learning/TEACHING_MAP.md).
