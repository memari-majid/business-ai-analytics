# AI in business analytics by application

Use this guide to find recent peer-reviewed work on a decision your team or students can specify and measure. It includes predictive models used in operating decisions, generative AI used in workflows, and evaluations of how those systems are deployed. Scite and publisher records were checked November 18, 2026. Citation years follow journal issues when assigned.

## Marketing and customer research

- **Choose which creative to test.** In a WhatsApp field experiment with one e-commerce brand, users were randomized among AI-personalized video, personalized image, and generic video ads. The AI-personalized videos raised engagement by six to nine percentage points relative to the comparison groups. The test changes several aspects of the ads across the three groups. A follow-up that isolates AI production would hold format and personalization fixed and measure incremental purchases and contribution after creative costs. Kapoor, A., & Kumar, M. (2025). Frontiers: Generative AI and personalized video advertisements. *Marketing Science*, *44*(4), 733–747. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2023.0494) · [Public MIT research brief](https://ide.mit.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/RB-DRAFT__7-8-25.pdf)
- **Decide where to use AI in customer discovery.** A replication of qualitative and quantitative research with a food company compared human, model, and hybrid approaches. Expert judges favored the hybrid in the tasks studied, and examples and retrieved context improved some synthetic responses. Use the design to compare coding, theme generation, and hypothesis development on actual interview transcripts. Validate any claim about customer preferences against a separate human sample before changing a product or campaign. Arora, N., Chakraborty, I., & Nishimura, Y. (2025). AI–human hybrids for marketing research: Leveraging large language models (LLMs) as collaborators. *Journal of Marketing*, *89*(2), 43–70. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429241276529)

Practice with incremental profit in the [campaign decision lab](../labs/01_campaign_decision.ipynb) and compare approaches to generalizing across creative in the [marketing research guide](RESEARCH.md#marketing-and-experimentation).

## Sales

- **Choose how to introduce an AI recommendation system.** A multinational pharmaceutical company tested the same system with different combinations of procedures, decision authority, training, and incentives. A difference-in-differences analysis estimated more client meetings when the design matched users' working styles and fewer meetings for the untailored design relative to the legacy system. The study also assessed units sold as a secondary measure. The authors caution about causal interpretation given the small number of clusters. A new pilot can randomize one change at a time or compare clearly defined bundles across enough comparable teams. Krakowski, S. M., Haftor, D., Luger, J., Pashkevich, N., & Raisch, S. (2026). Human-centered artificial intelligence: A field experiment. *Management Science*, *72*(1), 57–72. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2022.03849) · [Open Uppsala repository copy](https://uu.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2%3A1991857/FULLTEXT01.pdf)

## Operations and supply chain

- **Decide when planners may override a forecast.** A spare-parts retailer ran a field experiment that let merchants modify the forecasts feeding its inventory algorithm. Profitability was 4.92% higher on average with overrides than with automated forecasts alone in the studied setting. The evidence concerns the retailer's override policy and inputs. In another category, log override reasons and assess costs, stockouts, and profit on the affected items. Kesavan, S., Kushwaha, T., & Steele, D. (2026). Profit implications of judgmental adjustments to forecast inputs: Evidence from a large-scale field experiment. *Management Science*, *72*(1), 119–127. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2024.06321)
- **Allocate fulfillment capacity when demand surges.** Flipkart's implementation case describes a probabilistic forecaster and a stochastic optimizer that allocate capacity, with a separate rule that revises allocations as demand arrives. The case reports historical backtests, shadow comparisons, and tests of new policies. The platform changed multiple processes, so company-wide improvements cannot be assigned to the forecasting model alone. Use Figure 5 to trace the forecast through the capacity and service-level constraints. Agarwal, S., Agrawal, P., Allamsetty, A., Attavar, A., B, D., Bellala, G., Bhatnagar, S., Choudhari, H., Goel, V., Gupta, P., Kachroo, A., Kothadiya, J., KM, N., Kudupudi, S. A. K., Kumar, M., KVM, N., Modi, T., Moorthy, R., Nair, R. S., … Vyas, P. (2026). Faster, smarter, leaner: How Flipkart optimized its supply chain to unlock growth. *INFORMS Journal on Applied Analytics*, *56*(1), 42–57. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/inte.2025.0282)

The [inventory decision lab](../labs/02_inventory_decision.py) uses fictional demand to compare how models rank on forecast error and inventory cost.

## Finance, risk, and accounting

- **Audit the drivers of a credit model.** Using car-loan data, the authors apply a Shapley-based decomposition of AUC to identify input features that contribute to default ranking. Features that drive overall ranking gains can differ from features that explain individual predictions. Run a separate calibration and subgroup error analysis at thresholds used in the loan policy before drawing conclusions about how the policy affects applicants. Hué, S., Hurlin, C., Pérignon, C., & Saurin, S. (2026). Measuring the driving forces of predictive performance: Application to credit scoring. *Management Science*, *72*(11), 10156–10172. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2023.02025)
- **Check whether past earnings forecasts were truly out of sample.** Levy compares financial language models trained with different data cutoffs and tests arithmetic with and without tools. In the earnings task studied, apparent predictive performance can be explained by information from after the forecast date. Evaluate forecasts prospectively or use a model and inputs with auditable cutoffs. Recompute material numbers from source filings. Levy, B. (2026). Caution ahead: Numerical reasoning and look-ahead bias in AI models. *Journal of Accounting Research*, *64*(3), 1139–1188. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/1475-679X.70058)

For transaction classification and human review, see Choi and Xie's experiment in the [research guide](RESEARCH.md#organizations-and-real-work).

## Customer service

- **Decide when to show an agent suggested replies.** A meal-delivery company randomized customer-service agents' access to AI suggestions. Efficiency and customer sentiment improved for some conversations, including cancellations. In chats following a chatbot comprehension failure, subsequent assistance for the human agent could worsen sentiment. Review resolution, repeat contacts, and escalation separately for each reason the customer contacted support. Zhang, S., & Narayandas, D. (2026). Engaging customers with AI in online chats: Evidence from a randomized field experiment. *Management Science*, *72*(1), 73–95. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2022.03920)
- **Choose whether customers can bypass chatbot triage.** In incentivized online experiments, users chose chatbots less often than a model that minimizes expected service time predicted. The prospect of a transfer after an unsuccessful initial interaction contributed to that reluctance. Measure abandonment and time to resolution in your own queues before using a simulation to reallocate staff. Kagan, E., Dada, M., & Hathaway, B. (2026). Why are customers averse to service chatbots? *Manufacturing & Service Operations Management*, *28*(6), 1983–2003. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/msom.2024.1141)

Use the [grounded policy Q&A lab](../labs/03_grounded_policy_qa.py) to specify when a support system should answer, abstain, or escalate.

## Product and people

- **Decide where designers should use generation.** In controlled studies with students and professional designers, AI assistance improved creative work during ideation. During implementation it improved creativity for less experienced designers and reduced efficiency without a creativity gain for experts. Evaluate a specific product workflow by stage and include both idea quality and rework. Hou, J., Wang, L., Wang, G., Wang, H. J., & Yang, S. (2025). The double-edged roles of generative AI in the creative process: Experiments on design work. *Information Systems Research*. Advance online publication. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/isre.2024.0937)
- **Decide whether to expand access to a coding assistant.** Three randomized field experiments across companies exposed 4,867 software developers to different access conditions. The pooled estimate was a 26.08% increase in completed tasks associated with tool use, with a 10.3 percentage-point standard error and variation across the experiments. Evaluate delivered functionality, review and rework time, and defects alongside task counts before translating the result into capacity or hiring decisions. Cui, K. Z., Demirer, M., Jaffe, S., Musolff, L., Peng, S., & Salz, T. (2026). The effects of generative AI on high-skilled work: Evidence from three field experiments with software developers. *Management Science*, *72*(11), 9408–9420. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2025.00535)

## Compare two studies before applying either result

Write down the decision owner, eligible population, unit of assignment or observation, incumbent workflow, outcome window, and full costs. Compare studies that answer the same decision question with different data or approaches. For example, compare a retailer's randomized override experiment with Flipkart's deployment case. The first estimates the value of allowing overrides in that setting. The second shows how forecasting and optimization connect to capacity limits and operating controls.

Use the [research worksheet](RESEARCH.md#a-reusable-reading-worksheet) to record the comparison or the [field map](../learning/FIELD_MAP.md) to find cases and exercises for a different function.
