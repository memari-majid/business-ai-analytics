# Journals for AI in business analytics

Choose a venue by the claim you want to investigate. **Management Science, Information Systems Research, and MIS Quarterly** are useful anchors for business decisions, digital systems, and organizational outcomes. Use the specialty journals to find work with the relevant setting and methods. This guide prioritizes fit to the research question.

[Research papers](RESEARCH.md) · [State of the art](STATE_OF_THE_ART.md) · [Researchers and labs](PEOPLE.md) · [Topic index](../learning/TOPIC_INDEX.md)

## Core business and information systems

| Journal | What to look for | Audience and typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| [Management Science](https://pubsonline.informs.org/journal/mnsc) | Business effects of AI, firm and platform decisions, pricing, and analytics methods with practical consequences | Management and analytics scholars. Field experiments, empirical analysis, analytical models, and optimization. |
| [Information Systems Research](https://pubsonline.informs.org/journal/isre) | AI and digital platforms, economic value of information systems, and building and evaluating technology in organizations | Information systems and digital business researchers. Empirical, design, theoretical, and analytical work. [Editorial scope](https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/isre/editorial-statement) |
| [MIS Quarterly](https://aisel.aisnet.org/misq/) | Technology adoption, responsible design, governance, digital work, and organizational or societal effects | Information systems scholars. Research that develops theory or evaluates technology across users, firms, or institutions. |

## Marketing and customer analytics

| Journal | What to look for | Audience and typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| [Marketing Science](https://pubsonline.informs.org/journal/mksc) | Ad incrementality, customer response, targeting, marketplaces, demand estimation, and pricing | Quantitative marketing researchers. Econometric and structural models, experiments, and analytical work. |
| [Journal of Marketing Research](https://www.ama.org/journal-of-marketing-research/) | Marketing measurement, customer behavior, how creative affects response, and empirical designs for marketing decisions | Marketing researchers across quantitative and behavioral methods. Experiments, observational studies, and measurement methods. |

## Operations, forecasting, and applied analytics

| Journal | What to look for | Audience and typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| [Operations Research](https://pubsonline.informs.org/journal/opre) | Optimization, stochastic models, queues, and decisions under uncertainty | Operations research and decision science researchers. Mathematical models, proofs, algorithms, and computational evaluation. |
| [Manufacturing & Service Operations Management](https://pubsonline.informs.org/journal/msom) | Inventory, supply chains, service capacity, fulfillment, and prediction linked to operating decisions | Operations management scholars. Analytical, empirical, and computational work tied to operating processes. |
| [International Journal of Forecasting](https://forecasters.org/ijf/) | Probabilistic forecasts, forecast competitions, retail demand, forecast evaluation, and implementation | Forecasting researchers and practitioners. Compare forecasts at the relevant horizon and inspect the validation design and error measures. |
| [Journal of Business Analytics](https://www.tandfonline.com/jba) | Applied modeling methods and case studies with real-world data | Business analytics researchers and practitioners. Look for reproducible comparisons and how the study connects outputs to a decision. |

## Strategy, organizations, economics, and accounting

| Journal | What to look for | Audience and typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| [Organization Science](https://pubsonline.informs.org/journal/orsc) | Human–AI teamwork, expertise, work design, coordination, and organizational learning | Organization researchers. Field experiments, observational and qualitative studies, and theory. |
| [Strategic Management Journal](https://sms.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/10970266/homepage/productinformation.html) | AI and firm capabilities, organizational adaptation, resource allocation, and competitive advantage | Strategy researchers. Empirical, qualitative, computational, and theoretical studies of firm decisions and outcomes. |
| [The Quarterly Journal of Economics](https://academic.oup.com/qje/pages/About) | Productivity, labor, technology adoption, and economic mechanisms behind observed effects | Economists. Look for credible identification, the setting and comparison group, and who experiences the effects. |
| [Journal of Accounting Research](https://www.chicagobooth.edu/research/chookaszian/journal-of-accounting-research) | AI-assisted accounting work, disclosure, controls, and audit or financial-information quality | Accounting researchers. Archival, experimental, and analytical approaches to how financial information is created and used. |

## Statistical and machine learning methods

| Journal | What to look for | Audience and typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| [The Econometrics Journal](https://academic.oup.com/ectj/pages/About) | Causal machine learning, estimation, inference, and what changes when assumptions fail | Applied econometricians and methodologists. Theory, simulations, and applications with attention to identification and inference. |
| [Journal of Machine Learning Research](https://www.jmlr.org/) | Modeling methods, evaluation protocols, benchmark design, and reproducibility | Machine learning researchers. Read the experimental setup and assess how the data, baselines, and tasks relate to your business setting. Journal articles are openly accessible. |

## Other publication types

| Source | What it helps you find | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Working paper servers and preprint archives | Early results, public methods, and replication files. [NBER Working Papers](https://www.nber.org/papers) · [arXiv Artificial Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/recent) | Record the version and review status. Search the exact title and authors for a published version, then compare the methods and findings. |
| Conference and workshop proceedings | Technical advances and emerging benchmarks, often before journal articles. [Proceedings of Machine Learning Research](https://proceedings.mlr.press/) | Name the venue and track. Check the review process, dataset, baselines, and whether the setting supports the claim you are making. |
| Practitioner outlets | Management questions, implementation patterns, and links to case studies or research. [Harvard Business Review](https://hbr.org/topic/subject/ai-and-machine-learning) · [Knowledge at Wharton](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/ai-in-focus-series/) | Use practical accounts to frame a problem. For an empirical claim, trace the cited study and check the design and uncertainty. |

**Compare across venues.** For human–AI work, pair *Generative AI at Work* in *QJE* with *Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier* in *Organization Science*. For each one, record the task, who was studied, how AI access was assigned or introduced, the outcome, and where the effect varied. For marketing, compare *Generative Artificial Intelligence and Sales Productivity* in *Management Science* with *Evaluating Novel Unstructured Treatments with Generative AI* in *JMR*. The [paper guide](RESEARCH.md) links all four and summarizes their evidence.
