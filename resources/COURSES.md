# Courses for learning and teaching AI in business analytics

Selected for applied work, quality of public materials, and fit with a learner who already knows machine learning and causal inference. Access and published prices were checked on November 18, 2026. **Open materials** means the linked notes, syllabi, or assignments are public. **Free course**, **free audit**, and **free preview** reflect the different access terms published by each provider.

## Quick picks by topic

- **Marketing analytics and text intelligence.** Use Eccles MKTG 4640 as a classroom model. Complete its review-analysis project with a simple baseline and a modern model from the Hugging Face course. Compare errors before recommending a service or marketing change.
- **AI workflows and evaluation.** Read BYU Marriott's “Models in Your Product,” then work through Stanford CS329Z's November 4 and 11 evaluation lectures. Build a small test set with correct, ambiguous, and unsupported business requests.
- **Evidence for adoption.** Review the Google/Udacity A/B testing lessons on metrics and design. Continue with Stanford's advanced treatment-effect and policy-learning notes when the rollout question varies by segment.

## Experimentation and causal decision-making

| Course | Use it for | Access and source |
|---|---|---|
| **Google and Udacity · A/B Testing** | Start with Lessons 3–5 for metric selection, experiment design, and result interpretation. The five-lesson sequence models a concise introduction for business students. | **Free course**, sign-in for learning platform. [Official course outline](https://www.udacity.com/course/ab-testing--ud257) |
| **University of Utah Eccles · Algorithms for Business Decisions (MKTG 6600)** | Study a past Eccles graduate syllabus for randomized tests, observational methods, and treatment-effect heterogeneity. Use the syllabus to see how estimands and assumptions fit a manager's decision. The current program catalog describes Causal Analytics I and II. | **Open syllabus**, Fall 2025. [Historical syllabus](https://class-tools.app.utah.edu/syllabus/1258/17063/Syllabus%2Bfor%2BMKTG%2B6600-001%2BFall%2B2025%2BBus%2BDecision%2BAlgorithm.pdf) · [Current Eccles MSBA curriculum](https://eccles.utah.edu/programs/master-of-science-in-business-analytics/academics/) |
| **Stanford · Causal Inference (STATS 361 / MGTECON 661)** | Work through doubly robust estimation, heterogeneous treatment effects, and policy learning. Best advanced route for answering who should receive an intervention and whether a targeted policy improves outcomes. | **Open instructor notes and draft textbook.** The campus course assumes graduate statistics. [Stanford course listing](https://bulletin.stanford.edu/courses/stats-361) · [Instructor materials](https://web.stanford.edu/~swager/teaching.html) |
| **HarvardX · Causal Diagrams: Draw Your Assumptions Before Your Conclusions** | Use Miguel Hernán's graph-based lessons to teach confounding, selection bias, and adjustment. Transfer the examples to customer churn or campaign exposure after working through the original health and social-science cases. | **Free audit** for selected materials. Verified certificate listed at **$149**. [Harvard Online](https://harvardonline.harvard.edu/course/causal-diagrams-draw-your-assumptions-your-conclusions) |

## Text models, analytics agents, and AI products

| Course | Use it for | Access and source |
|---|---|---|
| **Hugging Face · LLM Course** | Use the pipelines lesson for classification, zero-shot labeling, and feature extraction. Continue with the token-classification lesson to practice extracting named entities. Pair with a labeled business dataset and a simple baseline. | **Free course** with public lessons and code. [Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/llm-course/chapter1/1) · [Pipelines](https://huggingface.co/learn/llm-course/en/chapter1/3) · [Token classification](https://huggingface.co/learn/llm-course/en/chapter7/2) |
| **Stanford · Engineering AI Agents (CS329Z)** | Best technical course in this list for evaluating AI workflows. Start with the **November 4** benchmark design lecture and **November 11** grading and reliability lecture. The project instructions require a meaningful evaluation of a system and its failure modes. | **Open materials**, Fall 2026 slides, selected videos and project instructions. [Course schedule](https://cs329z.stanford.edu/) · [Project](https://cs329z.stanford.edu/project.html) |
| **DeepLearning.AI and Snowflake · Building and Evaluating Data Agents** | Build a planner and execution workflow over structured and unstructured data. Compare answer grounding with trace-level checks of the agent's plan and actions. The examples use LangGraph and Snowflake. | **Limited-time free access advertised** by the provider, with graded assessment on Pro. Intermediate, about two hours. [Course and lesson outline](https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/building-and-evaluating-data-agents) |
| **BYU Marriott · Product Management (MSB 341 / IS 693R)** | Best public model here for teaching AI as a business-school studio. Read “Finding and Validating the Problem,” “Models in Your Product,” and the assessed sprints. The companion AI Foundry guide shows how a client practicum staffs teams, evaluates students, and delivers working software. | **Open course book and assignments**, Fall 2026. Practice with specific tools may require separate access. [Course book](https://byu-strategy.github.io/product-management/) · [Assessments](https://byu-strategy.github.io/product-management/00-assessments.html) · [AI Foundry guide](https://byu-strategy.github.io/ai-foundry/) |
