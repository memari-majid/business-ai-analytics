## Books

### Evals for AI Engineers

**Shreya Shankar and Hamel Husain · O’Reilly · October 2026.** Commercial book with a public contents page. The error-analysis, human annotation, and automated evaluator chapters help studio teams turn observed failures into a grading rubric and repeatable checks. [Publisher and contents](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/evals-for-ai/9798341660717/)

### Co-Existence

**Ethan Mollick · Portfolio · October 2026.** Commercial book with a public companion site. This follow-up to his 2024 bestseller *Co-Intelligence* is a business-facing read on how people and organizations might work with AI. Pair it with the [field studies](RESEARCH.md#organizations-and-real-work) when assessing productivity or adoption claims. [Publisher](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/813026/co-existence-by-ethan-mollick/) · [Author companion](https://co-existence.ai/classic/)

### Business Analytics and Artificial Intelligence: Concepts, Skills and Real World Application

**Zabihollah Rezaee, Joseph H. Zhang, Salem Lotfi Boumediene, and Nick J. Rezaee · Routledge · first edition released October 2026, copyright 2027.** Commercial book with a preview. Chapters 6, 8, and 12–14 offer a broad survey of strategy, prescriptive analytics, industry uses, responsible AI, and organizational transformation. For a project on testing agents, pair the survey with the evaluation resources on this page. [Publisher and contents](https://www.routledge.com/Business-Analytics-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Concepts-Skills-and-Real-World-Application/Rezaee-Zhang-Boumediene-Rezaee/p/book/9781041124009) · [Publisher release listing](https://www.routledge.com/economics-finance-business-industry/business-management-and-marketing/research-methods-in-management)

### Speech and Language Processing

**Daniel Jurafsky and James H. Martin · third-edition draft · August 19, 2026 release.** Free online manuscript and selected slides. Chapters 4 and 5 support classification baselines and embeddings. Chapter 11 covers information retrieval and retrieval-augmented generation, useful for a studio project answering questions with source evidence. [Authors’ edition and chapter downloads](https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3/)

### The Agentic Enterprise

**Babak Hodjat and Antoine Blondeau · O’Reilly · July 2026.** Commercial book with a public contents page. Use the chapters on the business case and return on investment to compare build-versus-buy decisions, pilot costs, and the work required to expand a deployment. The enterprise examples provide discussion cases. [Publisher and contents](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/the-agentic-enterprise/0642572274566/)

## Open course materials

### Product Management · BYU Marriott

**Scott Murff · MSB 341 / IS 693R / CS 498R · Fall 2026, course site published September 14, 2026.** Free course book and assignments. Following its hands-on tooling may require paid access. Use “Models in Your Product” for retrieval, tools, costs, and evals. Use “Measuring What Matters” to connect a product feature to business outcomes. [Course site](https://byu-strategy.github.io/product-management/) · [Models in Your Product](https://byu-strategy.github.io/product-management/11-models-in-your-product.html) · [Measuring What Matters](https://byu-strategy.github.io/product-management/14-measuring-what-matters.html)

### Engineering AI Agents · Stanford

**Diyi Yang, Michael Ryan, and John Yang · CS 329Z · Fall 2026.** Public schedule, selected slides, and project brief. The November 4 session gives students a structure for specifying the task, environment, stopping conditions, and scoring. The November 11 session covers judge design and reliability. The project brief shows how to require evidence that a proposal succeeds in a realistic setting. [Schedule and slides](https://cs329z.stanford.edu/) · [Project brief](https://cs329z.stanford.edu/project.html)

### Marketing Text Analytics · University of Utah Eccles

**Promothesh Chatterjee · MKTG 4640 · Spring 2026.** Public syllabus and project brief. The Yelp restaurant project turns sentiment, topics, and customer segments into recommendations about service, menus, and promotions. It provides a local model for asking students to defend a business decision with evidence from text. [Official syllabus and project](https://class-tools.app.utah.edu/syllabus/1264/12473/MKTG%2B4640%2BSyllabus.pdf#page=8)

## Practice and institutional resources

### Fall 2026 AI & Analytics Accelerator Projects

**Wharton AI & Analytics Initiative · Fall 2026.** Public sponsor briefs. Expedia’s brief asks for an evaluator for agent-generated travel text and images. RxSense’s brief asks students to forecast returns on AI investments. Use either as a model for writing a studio brief with deliverables, evaluation criteria, and a decision owner. The program arranges sponsor data access for participants. [Project briefs](https://ai-analytics.wharton.upenn.edu/for-students/ai-analytics-accelerator/#fall-2026-ai-analytics-accelerator-projects)

### The Adaptive Advantage: Who’s Winning With AI and How

**Wharton Human-AI Research and GBK Collective · October 13, 2026.** Public report summary and download. The survey compares what leaders and employees report about adoption, training, agents, and returns. Use the results to choose survey measures for a case discussion. Pair any effectiveness claim with evidence from a field experiment. [Report and summary](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/special-report/2026-ai-adoption-report/)

### The Enterprise AI Playbook: Lessons from 51 Successful Deployments

**Elisa Pereira, Alvin Wang Graylin, and Erik Brynjolfsson · Stanford Digital Economy Lab · April 2, 2026.** Free report. Cases of successful implementations show how teams redesigned workflows, tracked value, and assigned human oversight. Use the cases to develop pilot evaluation criteria. The case selection supports learning from successful examples. [Report and PDF](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/publication/enterprise-ai-playbook/)

### Demystifying evals for AI agents

**Mikaela Grace, Jeremy Hadfield, Rodrigo Olivares, and Jiri De Jonghe · Anthropic Engineering · January 9, 2026.** Free engineering guide. Its distinctions between outcome checks and trace inspection are useful for scoring a studio prototype. Give students examples with stale data, incorrect figures, or actions outside their permission. Ask them to define what should happen and build a test for each case. [Engineering guide](https://www.anthropic.com/research/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents)

### Meridian Open-Source

**Google Meridian team · public release January 29, 2025.** Free open-source library, documentation, and examples. The calibration and budget optimization notebooks offer an applied marketing analytics exercise. Have students explain how experiment inputs influence channel estimates before they recommend a budget. [Release announcement](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/meridian-marketing-mix-model-open-to-everyone/) · [Official notebooks](https://developers.google.com/meridian/notebook)
