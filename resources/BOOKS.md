# Books for learning and teaching AI in business analytics

Verified November 18, 2026. Dates refer to the linked editions or drafts, dated 2023 onward. **Free** means the linked site offers the full text. **Commercial** means the publisher offers a purchase, subscription, or institutional access. Preview links are identified separately.

For a shortlist of books and courses released since January 2025, see [recent resources](RECENT_RESOURCES.md).

## Quick picks by use

- **Decision economics.** Start with chapter 5 of *Machine Learning for Business Analytics* and the churn case in Huntsinger's *Business Analytics*. Use the [campaign tutorial](../labs/01_campaign_decision.ipynb) to connect measured incremental lift, margin, and campaign cost.
- **Experimentation and causal inference.** Start with Dong's chapters on experimental design and randomized field experiments. For a causal ML extension, use the BERT and double machine learning notebook from *Applied Causal Inference Powered by ML and AI*.
- **Bayesian analysis.** Work through chapter 2 of Martin's *Bayesian Analysis with Python* on posterior-based decisions and predictive checks. Use Dong's marketing examples to practice updating beliefs when new customer data arrives.
- **AI workflow design and evaluation.** Read chapters 3 and 6 of *AI Engineering* and chapters 3, 5, and 8 of *Evals for AI Engineers*. Use the latter to add a failure taxonomy and regression tests to the AI analyst exercise.
- **Business-school survey.** Rezaee and colleagues survey applications, governance, and transformation. Sharda, Delen, and Turban provide a managerial analytics textbook, and Huntsinger provides decision cases.
- **Business audience.** *Strategic Analytics* connects quantitative methods to competition and managerial planning. Mollick's *Co-Intelligence* and *Co-Existence* offer practitioner perspectives on human–AI collaboration and how it may change work.

## Experiments and causal machine learning

| Book | What to read and use | Edition and access |
|---|---|---|
| **Xiaojing Dong, *Marketing Analytics and Data Science: Tools and Models*** | Read the chapters on Bayesian updating and experimental design and on causal analysis with a randomized field experiment. Apply them to a campaign experiment that defines the outcome, assignment unit, and decision threshold in advance. | First edition, May 2026. Commercial, with public chapter descriptions. [Palgrave Macmillan](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-032-11130-2) |
| **Victor Chernozhukov, Christian Hansen, Nathan Kallus, Martin Spindler, and Vasilis Syrgkanis, *Applied Causal Inference Powered by ML and AI*** | Chapter 5 covers identification with observed controls, chapter 10 includes the DoubleML notebook using BERT features, and chapters 14–15 cover heterogeneous effects and validation. [Author site](https://causalml-book.org/) |
| **Matheus Facure, *Causal Inference in Python*** | Use the sections on evaluating conditional average treatment effects, cumulative gain, and decisions to compare targeting by predicted response with targeting by estimated incremental effect. | July 2023. | **Osvaldo Martin, *Bayesian Analysis with Python*** | Chapter 2 uses posterior distributions for decisions and predictive checks.
