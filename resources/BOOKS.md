# Books for learning and teaching AI in business analytics

Verified November 18, 2026. **Free** means the linked site offers the full text. **Commercial** means the publisher offers a purchase, subscription, or institutional access. Preview links are identified separately. Dates refer to the editions or drafts linked below.

## Quick picks by use

- **Decision economics.** Read chapters 7 and 8 of *Data Science for Business*, then use them to translate the campaign tutorial's lift into expected profit.
- **Experimentation and causal inference.** Read chapters 2, 7, and 21 of *Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments*. For an advanced causal extension, use the BERT and double machine learning notebook from *Applied Causal Inference Powered by ML and AI*.
- **AI workflow design and evaluation.** Read chapters 3 and 6 of *AI Engineering* and chapters 3, 5, and 8 of *Evals for AI Engineers*. Use the latter to add a failure taxonomy and regression tests to the AI analyst exercise.
- **Business-school survey.** Rezaee and colleagues survey applications, governance, and transformation. Sharda, Delen, and Turban provide a managerial analytics textbook, and Huntsinger provides decision cases.
- **Business audience.** *Prediction Machines* introduces AI economics. *Co-Intelligence*, a bestseller, offers a practitioner perspective on human–AI collaboration.
