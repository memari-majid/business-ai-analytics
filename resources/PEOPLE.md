# People in AI and business analytics

Representative researchers and practitioners grouped by contribution. Each entry points to a public profile or collection and a paper, book, course, or guide that shows the person's work. For evidence by topic and study design, see the [research guide](RESEARCH.md).

## Which author for which question?

| Question | Author and entry point |
|---|---|
| Where does AI create economic value in a decision? | **Ajay Agrawal, Joshua Gans, and Avi Goldfarb**, *Prediction Machines* for the link between prediction, judgment, and action. [Book](https://store.hbr.org/product/prediction-machines-updated-and-expanded-the-simple-economics-of-artificial-intelligence/10598) · [The authors' 2026 update](https://www.rotman.utoronto.ca/the-rotman-experience/about-rotman/ai-at-rotman/ai-era-management-matters-more/understanding-ai-prediction-machine/) |
| How do I turn a model score into a profitable decision? | **Foster Provost and Tom Fawcett**, *Data Science for Business* for cost-sensitive thresholds, expected value, and lift curves. [Book](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/data-science-for/9781449374273/) |
| How do I test whether a business intervention works? | **Ron Kohavi, Diane Tang, and Ya Xu** for online experiment design. **Susan Athey** for estimating effects and choosing interventions that vary across groups. [Experiment Guide](https://experimentguide.com/) · [Policy learning](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/working-papers/efficient-policy-learning) |
| Where should marketers apply predictive or generative AI? | **Rajkumar Venkatesan and Jim Lecinski**, *The AI Marketing Canvas* for choosing use cases and scaling adoption. **Paul Ellickson** and colleagues give an empirical example of evaluating proposed marketing content. [The AI Marketing Canvas](https://www.darden.virginia.edu/node/34946) · [Marketing study](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437261476639) |
| How should people work with generative AI? | **Ethan Mollick**, *Co-Intelligence* for collaboration practices. **Erik Brynjolfsson, Danielle Li, and Lindsey Raymond** for measured effects from a workplace rollout. [Book](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/741805/co-intelligence-by-ethan-mollick/9780593716717/) · [Field study](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjae044) |
| How do I evaluate an AI workflow before deploying it? | **Shreya Shankar and Hamel Husain** for task-level errors and evaluator checks. **Chip Huyen** for how evaluation, retrieval, and operating cost shape the system. [Evals for AI Engineers](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/evals-for-ai/9798341660717/) · [AI Engineering](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/ai-engineering/9781098166298/) |

The [book catalog](BOOKS.md) lists editions, access options, and suggested chapters.

## Business analytics, causal inference, and experimentation

| Person | Contribution to explore | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| **Susan Athey** · Stanford Graduate School of Business | Connects econometrics and machine learning through treatment-effect estimation and policy learning. Her work helps answer who should receive an intervention when average effects hide differences across groups. | [Faculty research and publications](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/faculty/susan-athey) · [Efficient Policy Learning](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/working-papers/efficient-policy-learning) |
| **Ron Kohavi** | Co-authored a practical reference on online controlled experiments. His work on experiment platforms connects random assignment, metric integrity, and rollout decisions at scale. | [Publications and talks](https://ai.stanford.edu/~ronnyk/) · [Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments](https://experimentguide.com/) |
| **Foster Provost** · NYU Stern | Connects predictive modeling with the value of decisions. The expected-value and lift chapters of his book with Tom Fawcett show how to evaluate a model against a real intervention cost. | [Faculty profile](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/faculty/bio/foster-provost) · [Data Science for Business](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/data-science-for/9781449374273/) |

## Text intelligence and marketing

| Person | Contribution to explore | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| **Paul Ellickson** · University of Rochester Simon Business School | Uses quantitative marketing and causal inference to study firm decisions. His co-authored work on novel unstructured treatments combines text representations and historical causal evidence to evaluate proposed marketing content. | [Faculty profile](https://simon.rochester.edu/faculty/paul-ellickson) · [Evaluating Novel Unstructured Treatments with Generative AI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437261476639) |
| **Dan Jurafsky** · Stanford University | Co-authors the open *Speech and Language Processing* draft with James H. Martin. The text supplies technical foundations for classifying, representing, and retrieving the unstructured records used in business analytics. | [Research and publications](https://stanford.edu/~jurafsky/) · [Open textbook](https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3/) |
| **Arul Mishra** · University of Utah David Eccles School of Business | Uses machine learning to study consumer decisions. In one co-authored study, the team traced how associations learned from text reached advertisements and recommendations and tested consumer responses. | [Faculty publications](https://profiles.faculty.utah.edu/u0571376/publications) · [Algorithms Propagate Gender Bias in the Marketplace](https://doi.org/10.1002/jcpy.1351) |
| **Himanshu Mishra** · University of Utah David Eccles School of Business | Applies machine learning and computational social science to consumer and marketplace behavior. His co-authored study on LLM-assisted hypothesis generation illustrates a research workflow where language models propose hypotheses for researchers to assess. | [Faculty profile](https://profiles.faculty.utah.edu/u0521228) · [Machine-Assisted Social Psychology Hypothesis Generation](https://doi.org/10.1037/amp0001222) |
| **Rajkumar Venkatesan** · University of Virginia Darden | Studies customer analytics and marketing return on investment. In the second edition of *The AI Marketing Canvas*, he and Jim Lecinski connect predictive and generative AI use cases with adoption and governance. | [Faculty profile](https://www.darden.virginia.edu/faculty-research/directory/rajkumar-venkatesan) · [The AI Marketing Canvas](https://www.darden.virginia.edu/node/34946) |

## Forecasting and operational decisions

| Person | Contribution to explore | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| **Dimitris Bertsimas** · MIT Sloan | Connects statistical learning with constrained optimization. The open *Analytics Edge* course gives concrete examples of turning predictions into resource-allocation decisions. | [Faculty profile](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/faculty/directory/dimitris-bertsimas) · [The Analytics Edge](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-071-the-analytics-edge-spring-2017/) |
| **Rob J. Hyndman** · Monash University | Co-authors *Forecasting: Principles and Practice* with George Athanasopoulos. It explains seasonal baselines, time-series validation, uncertainty, and forecasts across product or regional hierarchies. | [Research and software](https://robjhyndman.com/) · [Open textbook](https://otexts.com/fpp3/) |
| **Warren B. Powell** | Develops frameworks for sequential decisions under uncertainty. His inventory examples show how repeated decisions use forecasts, costs, constraints, and information observed over time. | [Sequential Decision Analytics and Modeling](https://warrenpowell.org/sdamodeling/) |

## AI value, work, and organizations

| Person | Contribution to explore | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| **Ajay Agrawal** · University of Toronto Rotman | Studies AI economics and innovation. In work with Joshua Gans and Avi Goldfarb, he explains how cheaper prediction changes the value of judgment and creates opportunities to redesign decisions. | [Faculty profile](https://www.rotman.utoronto.ca/the-rotman-experience/our-community/people/agrawal-ajay/) · [The authors' 2026 update to their prediction framework](https://www.rotman.utoronto.ca/the-rotman-experience/about-rotman/ai-at-rotman/ai-era-management-matters-more/understanding-ai-prediction-machine/) |
| **Erik Brynjolfsson** · Stanford Digital Economy Lab | Studies how digital technology affects productivity and work. His co-authored field paper on a customer-support assistant separates average productivity changes from variation across workers. | [Lab research profile](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/person/erik-brynjolfsson/) · [Generative AI at Work](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjae044) |
| **Karim R. Lakhani** · Harvard Business School | Studies digital transformation and how organizations redesign work around AI. His co-authored consulting experiment reports performance improvements on some tasks and losses on a task outside the tested tool's capabilities. | [Harvard profile](https://www.cityleadership.harvard.edu/about/person/karim-lakhani/) · [Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier](https://doi.org/10.1287/orsc.2025.21838) |
| **Ethan Mollick** · Wharton | Studies generative AI in work, entrepreneurship, and education. His co-authored experiment compares individual and team work with and without AI on product-development tasks. *Co-Intelligence* introduces practical ways for people to work with generative AI. | [Faculty profile](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/faculty/ethan-mollick/) · [The Cybernetic Teammate](https://doi.org/10.1287/orsc.2025.20702) · [Co-Intelligence](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/741805/co-intelligence-by-ethan-mollick/9780593716717/) |

## Production AI systems and evaluation

| Person | Contribution to explore | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| **Hamel Husain** | Publishes engineering practices for evaluating AI applications. His practitioner guide turns observed errors into production checks and explains how to assess a model-based grader against human labels. | [Your AI Product Needs Evals](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals/) · [Evals for AI Engineers](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/evals-for-ai/9798341660717/) |
| **Chip Huyen** | Writes about designing and operating machine learning systems. Her *AI Engineering* book connects evaluation, retrieval, cost, and latency to product architecture. | [Author site](https://huyenchip.com/) · [AI Engineering](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/ai-engineering/9781098166298/) |
| **Shreya Shankar** | Studies how people specify quality criteria and assess LLM outputs. Her co-authored paper on evaluator alignment examines how human preferences can inform and test automated evaluators. | [Research and publications](https://www.sh-reya.com/) · [Who Validates the Validators?](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~bjoern/papers/shankar-validators-uist2024.pdf) |
