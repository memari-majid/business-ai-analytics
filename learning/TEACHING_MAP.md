## A short class you could teach

**Can we prove the AI-written campaign created profitable lift?** Give students the campaign evidence and three draft recommendations, including one persuasive response with the wrong denominator. Have them estimate lift and uncertainty, incorporate the economics and unsubscribe threshold, then defend a recommendation. Change an assumption during the discussion and ask them what evidence would justify a different action.

Use a rubric that credits the estimand and baseline, traceable numbers, treatment of uncertainty, decision economics, and communication. Treat a recommendation that violates the guardrail as a decision error even if the calculations are correct. Compare [Eccles' public Marketing Text Analytics syllabus](https://class-tools.app.utah.edu/syllabus/1264/12473/MKTG%2B4640%2BSyllabus.pdf) with [BYU Marriott's Product Management course book](https://byu-strategy.github.io/product-management/) to see two different ways to teach application and evaluation.
